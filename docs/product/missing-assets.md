## Intake requirements

For every supplied file or claim, record:

1. Source owner and authorized approver.
2. Version/date and stable asset location.
3. Copyright, subject, client, outlet, and platform permissions.
4. Approved surfaces, crop/edit permissions, attribution, and expiration/review date.
5. Accessibility metadata such as caption/transcript and meaningful alt-text context.
6. Whether the item is public, staff-only, private-client, or restricted.

Do not accept approval conveyed only by a filename such as `final`. The named approval record in `content-inventory.md` is authoritative.

## Safe placeholder policy

- Use generic blocks labelled `Placeholder - content awaiting approval` in local/preview/staging only.
- Do not use invented people, client brands, numerical results, prices, dates, quotes, venues, handles, phone numbers, or email addresses.
- Synthetic test records must use reserved example domains and obviously non-production names.
- Production startup/import checks must not publish placeholder records.
